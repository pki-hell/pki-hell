> Abandon all *trust*, ye who enter here-- for in these vaults lie the sins of the cert, where broken chains and false assuances drag the unwary deeper; you think you hold the keys to trust, but the keys hold you.  Enter this place and see how every shortcut, every sin of cert, becomes another crack in the foundation of security. <br>


Public Key Infrastructure and Crytography can be dry topics at the best of times, and hell-on-earth in the worst of them. Follow Dante's journey to become familiar with crypto and PKI concepts, pitfalls, and utilities to guide one beyond hell, towards salvation.
