## **Bolgia 9: The Sowers of Discord**

This ditch is carnage. Souls are torn apart by demons with blades, their bodies split open. Yet each wound heals, only to be cut again.

*"These sowed division in the chain itself. They poisoned stores with rogue roots, split authority against authority, destroyed the unity of trust. Now their flesh is riven endlessly, as once they tore the fabric of the chain."*

His tone darkens.

*"Enterprises with uncontrolled internal roots, governments planting rogue CAs — each tore the body of trust apart. So their own bodies are torn in kind."*
