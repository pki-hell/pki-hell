## **Ring Two: The Forest of Suicide (Violence Against Self)**

You enter a blackened forest, each tree twisted in torment. A harpy lands on a branch and snaps it; blood flows from the wound, and a voice cries out in pain.

*"These are the admins who destroyed their own PKI. They lost root keys, deleted backups, burned their own authority out of ignorance or pride. Their bodies are gone; they live only as bleeding trees, each branch a broken key, each wound a lost system that can never be restored."*

The forest echoes with cries of despair, each branch another unrecoverable cert, another lost chain.
