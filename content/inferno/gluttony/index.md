# **Circle 3: Gluttony — The Mire of Excess**

The descent grows cold and wet. A foul stench rises as a ceaseless rain falls — not water, but a torrent of sodden scrolls, certificates inked and rotting in the storm. The ground beneath is a swamp of parchment and sludge, sucking at your feet. Souls lie half-buried, their bodies swollen and pale, their mouths filled with filth. Above them prowls a beast: **Cerberus**, three heads, each gnashing at the sinners, tearing mouthfuls of flesh as punishment for their hunger.

Virgil gestures, his expression grim.

*"This is the circle of Gluttony. Here lie those who gorged upon issuance, who flooded their realms with certs and attributes without measure. They thought abundance meant safety. But overfed trust grows sick, and now they lie wallowing in the waste of their excess."*

---

You see a soul trying to rise, but his flesh is bloated with glowing certs pressed into his skin. Each movement tears another free, spilling chains of ink that melt into the muck. The rain pelts him with more scrolls, piling higher until he sinks beneath.

*"These issued without limit — thousands upon thousands of certificates, scattered without governance, without lifecycle. They sought to feast on abundance, but they birthed only sprawl. When compromise came, they could not revoke; when audit came, they could not count. Now their endless certs bury them as surely as mud."*

---

Nearby, a soul claws at a pile of massive scrolls, each bearing the word **CRL** in bold. The scrolls are so swollen they burst, spilling ink that smothers him. Each time he claws free, more scrolls fall from the rain, heavier still.

*"These glutted their revocations — CRLs bloated beyond reason, thousands of entries, published rarely, unreadable by clients. They thought length meant diligence, but it brought only latency, failure, collapse. Now their scrolls choke them, endless, useless, heavy as stone."*

---

Another lies stretched across the mire, his body grotesquely tattooed. Names, domains, and roles are scrawled across his skin in glowing script — `CN=`, `SAN=`, `EKU=` etched over and over until his flesh is illegible. He moans as Cerberus tears another strip of writing from his side.

*"These filled their certs with excess — SANs unbounded, EKUs unchecked, attributes piled on until the cert was no cert at all, but a grotesque charter of all things. They sought to give everything, and gave nothing. Now their flesh is covered in fields, forever illegible."*

---

Deeper still, you see a soul bound in chains of cipher. His body is wrapped in endless encryption, layer upon layer, so tight that his chest cannot rise. He gasps silently as the weight crushes him down into the mire.

*"And these wrapped all things in excess. Triple encryption, needless signatures, chains so redundant they suffocated their own use. They thought strength lay in piling cipher upon cipher. But security without restraint is not security — it is death. Now they suffocate in chains they themselves forged."*

---

The storm grows heavier, drowning the cries in muck and sludge. The beast tears, the scrolls fall, the swamp consumes.

Virgil leans close, his words like iron.

*"Remember this: more is not more. To issue endlessly is to drown. To bloat revocation is to choke. To scrawl attributes without measure is to rot. And to over-encrypt is to suffocate. Balance is strength. Excess is Gluttony — and Gluttony is waste."*
