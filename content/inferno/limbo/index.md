# **Circle 1: Limbo — The Unanchored**

The path descends from the dark wood into a wide plain wrapped in twilight. A castle rises in the distance, its walls strong, its towers tall, but its gates barred with silence. The air is hushed, heavy with longing. Within, voices speak wisdom, but none beyond the walls hear.

Virgil raises his hand.

*"This is Limbo, the place of those who sought to be trusted, yet lacked the seal. They are not damned for malice, nor torn for betrayal — only denied. Their chains are incomplete, their anchors cut away. Thus, though noble, they wander here, untrusted forever."*

---

You approach the castle walls. Through the barred gates you see figures holding glowing scrolls, each bearing knowledge, each carefully written. But none can pass them beyond the gate; each scroll fades into dust as it touches the bars.

*"Behold the self-signed,"* Virgil says softly. *"They forged their own seals, but none bore witness. Their certs end in themselves, their trust circles back and dies. In solitude, they suffice — in isolation, they may serve. But no chain descends from them, and so no faith ascends to them. They are noble, but forever denied."*

---

Further within, you see souls dragging chains across the plain. Each chain glows, but links are missing. The souls reach endlessly toward a radiant tower — but the gaps in their chains leave them grasping at shadows.

*"These bore intermediates, yet not the root. They served half the chain, but never the whole. They did not publish their intermediates, and so their certs failed in the hands of strangers. The chain was broken, and so the trust was lost. Now they wander here, pulling chains that will never complete."*

---

Near the castle’s base lies a row of broken tablets. Each is etched faintly with words like `CN=Server`, yet none carry SANs, none bear EKUs. The souls clutch them desperately, pleading for entry, but the guards do not move.

*"And these denied purpose. Their certs lacked extensions, their SANs were empty, their EKUs void. No role could be known, no name could be trusted. They are not evil — but incomplete. And so they remain here, unheard, unseen, unanchored."*

---

Virgil’s voice carries a note of sorrow.

*"Know this, my student: Limbo is the softest circle, yet it is cruelest still. These souls suffer not flame or frost, but silence. They cry out, yet none believe. For in PKI, to lack anchoring, chaining, or purpose is to be noble yet useless — and thus to wander forever in Limbo."*
