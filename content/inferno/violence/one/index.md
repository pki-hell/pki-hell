## **Ring One: The River of Blood (Violence Against Others)**

*"Here flow the tyrants of keys — those who spilled blood with their negligence. They left private keys unguarded, or worse, allowed them to be stolen. Attackers forged their seals, and with those false certs they brought ruin upon others. Now the guilty boil in the very river of breaches they caused."*

Souls thrash in the river, clutching keys that glow red-hot, searing their hands. Every attempt to release them only drives the key deeper into the flesh.
