## **Bolgia 6: The Hypocrites**

A slow procession winds through this ditch. Souls wear cloaks gilded in gold, gleaming in the firelight. But their steps are labored, their faces crushed with pain. The cloaks are lined with lead, so heavy each step sinks deep into the earth.

*"These wore the golden padlock proudly, their sites adorned with symbols of safety, yet within their certs were broken — expired, weak, unfit for use. They deceived with appearance, hiding rot beneath. Now their cloaks shine but crush them into the ground."*

He shakes his head.

*"How many sites carried certificates long past expiration, or used TLS so weak it might as well have been none? They gleamed to the ignorant, but to those who looked close, they were leaden fraud. Their cloaks are lead still."*
