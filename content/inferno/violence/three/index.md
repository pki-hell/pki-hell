## **Ring Three: The Burning Desert (Violence Against God, Nature, Art)**

The forest thins, and you step onto burning sand that stretches to the horizon. Fire rains from the black sky, each ember a mathematical guess. Souls writhe on the sand, clutching brittle locks, their skin seared by the endless storm.

*"These are the blasphemers against cryptography itself — who clung to weak algorithms, who trusted RSA-512, DES, and MD5 even as they cracked and fell. Every grain of sand is a brute-force attempt, every ember a collision. They denied the strength of the art, and now the art consumes them."*

---

Virgil turns to you, the desert flames reflected in his eyes.

*"Violence in PKI takes many forms. Against others, by negligence; against oneself, by destruction; against the art itself, by denying its strength. Each leaves scars, each boils, bleeds, or burns. Mark well the lessons of this circle, lest you find your own keys in blood, your own tree in torment, or your own skin seared by the fire of weak ciphers."*
