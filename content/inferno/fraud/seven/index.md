## **Bolgia 7: The Thieves**

Serpents writhe through this trench, striking at souls who scream as their very shapes twist. Some vanish, replaced by the snakes themselves; others take on new forms entirely.

*"These stole keys, stole signatures, stole identities. They took certs not their own and wore them like masks. Code-signing stolen, chains forged, names corrupted. Now serpents steal their very bodies, endlessly as they once stole trust."*

He looks grim.

*"Think of Stuxnet — signed with stolen certs to appear true. Think of ransomware carrying the names of companies who never made them. Theft of trust is theft of self — and here, the self is stolen without end."*
