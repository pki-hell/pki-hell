## **Bolgia 1: The Panderers and Seducers**

Souls march endlessly in lines, driven back and forth by demons with whips. Their backs are flayed raw, their cries sharp against the stone.

*"These are the seducers of trust — vendors who dressed their works in false padlocks, who whispered promises of safety that were lies. They sold illusions of security, enticing the ignorant to follow. Now demons drive them without rest, whipped as they once whipped others into belief."*

He glances at you.

*"Think of the padlock in a browser bar — once sold as a symbol of safety, though it only meant ‘encrypted,’ never ‘honest.’ Many were led astray, believing a thief’s page secure because a lock shone beside its name. That is the sin of seduction — and this is its price."*
