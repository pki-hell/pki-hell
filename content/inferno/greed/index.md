# **Circle 4: Greed — The Weights of Dominion**

The rain and sludge fade as the path winds down to a wide ring lit by dim fire. Across it, souls strain under immense weights of iron, shoving them endlessly in circles. When they collide, they hurl curses at one another, then turn and push again, trapped forever in their toil. Their faces are twisted with hunger — not for food, but for possession.

Virgil lifts his hand.

*"This is the circle of Greed. Here are those who seized too much trust, who craved dominion over all names and domains, who sought to bind what could not be bound. They hoarded certs, they claimed all beneath their banners, they wasted as much as they amassed. Their hunger was endless, and so their toil is endless still."*

---

One soul heaves beneath a colossal scroll, the word `*.domain.com` etched across it in fire. The scroll drags behind him, vast and ragged, and each step grinds him deeper into the stone. When he falters, demons lash him until he staggers onward.

*"Behold the wildcards,"* Virgil says darkly. *"They were greedy to bind all subdomains with one cert, declaring all beneath their dominion. But their claim was folly, for within ‘all’ lurked their enemies, their shadows, their weakness. When the cert was compromised, every service fell with it. Now they bear its weight forever, heavy as their hunger."*

---

Nearby, two groups of souls push against one another, their weights crashing in the middle of the ring. One hoards certs, vaulting endless stacks beneath them; the other issues wildly, scattering certs in waste. They collide, screaming at each other — *“Why hoard?”* *“Why waste?”* — then turn and repeat the cycle without end.

*"These are the hoarders and the wasters,"* Virgil explains. *"Some locked certs away, issuing none, strangling their own trust. Others issued endlessly without restraint, flooding their realms in chaos. Both were greedy, though in opposite form. Both are punished here, locked in an argument that never ends."*

---

Farther along, you see a soul crowned in iron, bearing chains engraved with names: `CN=`, `OU=`, `O=`, hundreds wrapped around his neck. He clutches them desperately, his eyes wild. Each chain glows as if alive, then shatters, leaving him screaming. But new chains grow back in their place, and the cycle begins again.

*"These sought to control all attributes,"* Virgil says, almost with pity. *"They demanded every name, every field, every domain extension, thinking ownership was safety. They chained themselves to false authority. But in the end, the chain breaks, and the greed consumes its master."*

---

The sound of clashing weights echoes in the gloom, punctuated by curses, screams, and the lash of demons. The ring is a place of endless hunger, endless toil.

Virgil looks at you, his eyes grim.

*"Learn this: trust cannot be hoarded, nor can it be claimed in greed. To bind all beneath one banner is folly. To hoard certs is paralysis. To issue endlessly is chaos. Greed consumes itself, and those who sought dominion now roll its weight forever."*
