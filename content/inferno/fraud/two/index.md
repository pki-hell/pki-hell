## **Bolgia 2: The Flatterers**

Here the ditch stinks beyond bearing. Souls sink in excrement, clawing for air, but every movement only plunges them deeper.

*"These praised themselves, their ciphers, their schemes — ‘unbreakable,’ they boasted, ‘military grade,’ they swore. Yet their encryption was weakness draped in praise. Their words were filth, and so filth consumes them."*

He points to a soul writhing in the mire.

*"Snake-oil cryptography — closed, proprietary, full of boasts and empty of proofs. Many sold it, and many bought it. They all drowned in lies. So too do these, forever."*
