**Round 1: Caina (Traitors to Kin)**

Souls are buried up to their necks in the ice, their tears freezing to their faces.

*"These are those who betrayed their own domains — insiders who turned their access against their kin. They leaked internal roots, they exfiltrated intermediate keys, they planted backdoors. The family of trust relied on them, and they sold it out. Now they weep frozen tears, forever kinless."*

**Real world echo:** Insider admins leaking internal CA keys, or misusing enrollment rights.
