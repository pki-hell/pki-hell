## **Bolgia 5: The Corrupt Officials (Barrators)**

Here, boiling pitch bubbles and spits. Souls try to rise from the tar, but demons hook them with claws and thrust them back under.

*"These dipped their hands in man-in-the-middle. They forged chains to intercept, installed false roots to spy. Their trust was corruption, their authority bent for power. Now they drown in pitch, dragged down by demons with hooks of iron."*

He leans close.

*"You know them — the enterprises that install interception proxies, breaking TLS by forcing false trust roots into devices. They cloak themselves in authority, yet it is only fraud. The hooks will find them here."*
