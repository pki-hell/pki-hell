# **Circle 7: Violence \- The Rings**

The air grows hotter, the land harsher. You hear screams carried by a dry wind. Ahead lies a red river boiling with blood, its banks patrolled by demons with bows. Beyond it stretches a black forest, its trees twisted and writhing as though alive. And still farther, a desert of burning sand under a rain of fire, where souls writhe in agony, their flesh smoldering.

Virgil lifts his hand to each in turn.
