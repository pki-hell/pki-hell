# **Circle 2: Lust — The Storm of Secrets**

The descent narrows, the light dims, and you hear it before you see it: a roar like mountains tearing. A storm howls in the blackness, its winds carrying chains and torn fragments of glowing parchment. The air itself lashes at your skin as the storm surges past.

Virgil raises his cloak against the gale.

*"This is the circle of Lust — not of flesh, but of secrets. Here are those who gave away what should have been guarded, who bound many hands to one key, who let passion for ease rule over discipline. They thought it harmless. But trust betrayed by desire is trust forever lost. And so the storm carries them, never still, never safe."*

---

You glimpse souls in the whirlwind. Two figures cling desperately to each other, bound together by a single radiant private key. The wind jerks them apart; one slips, and the key is wrenched from both their hands, leaving them shrieking as they are flung into the darkness.

Virgil speaks, his tone sharp.

*"Behold the shared key — many lovers, one secret. As long as one faltered, all were betrayed. In life, admins shared root passwords, private certs, SSH keys, thinking convenience stronger than fidelity. But when the breach came, none could deny it, for none could prove it was not their hand. This is the fruit of their desire — a storm that never ends."*

---

Further on, another soul clutches tightly to a key glowing with age. Its edges are cracked, its light dim. Yet still he clings, refusing to let it go. The storm howls, and with every gust the key splinters further, until it is dust in the wind.

*"These lusted for comfort, and so they never rotated. They feared the pain of change, clung to secrets long expired, long compromised. They let TLS die on the vine, they let API keys persist beyond reason. Now the wind tears the keys from their grip, again and again, showing them what they should have cast aside."*

---

Then you see others flung naked through the gale, their hands reaching in vain for secrets scattered like ashes. Fragments of configuration files, lines of code, entire repos swirl through the storm, each glowing with private keys laid bare.

Virgil’s face hardens.

*"And these are the indiscriminate — those who exposed secrets in public. They posted keys to code repositories, to logs, to open shares, careless as lovers who parade their desire. Once revealed, their secrets could never be reclaimed. Now the storm scatters them without end, a testament to their folly."*

---

The wind presses against you, full of the stench of desperation. The storm itself feels alive, as if each gust were another soul’s failure made manifest.

Virgil leans close, his voice cutting through the roar.

*"Learn from what you see: never share what is meant to be singular. Never bind many hands to one key. Rotate without fear, for no secret may last forever. And never, never expose desire to the world. For those who lust for ease will find only the storm."*
