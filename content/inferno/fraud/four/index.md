## **Bolgia 4: The Diviners**

A grotesque sight: souls walk with their heads twisted backward. Their eyes weep as they stumble, forced to look only behind them.

*"These are the issuers of false names, who gave identities to those who never owned them. They placed domains in the hands of strangers, twisted subject fields, broke the bond between name and truth. Now their heads are twisted likewise, forever seeing truth only after it is lost."*

He sighs.

*"So too when CAs misissued, granting certs for domains not theirs — like when whole chains were poisoned by false entries. The damage is seen only after the fact, when it is too late to walk straight."*
