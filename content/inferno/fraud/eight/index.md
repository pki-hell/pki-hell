## **Bolgia 8: The Deceitful Counselors**

Flames move like living beings here. Each soul is wrapped in fire, their voices faint within the blaze.

*"These gave counsel of false security. They advised weak ciphers, encouraged roll-your-own crypto, whispered that broken was safe. Their tongues misled kings and companies alike. Now they are sealed in flames, their lies consuming them, their words never heard again."*

He glances at the flames.

*"How many consultants advised DES long past its death, or boasted of secret algorithms stronger than all? Their words burned others — now the fire burns them."*
