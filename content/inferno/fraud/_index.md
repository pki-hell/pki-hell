# **Circle 8: Fraud — The Ten Ditches of Malebolge**

The land cracks open into a vast chasm, its depths carved into ten circling ditches, each bridged by jagged stone. The air is heavy with deceit; the very ground seems slick with lies. From each bolgia rise screams — some shrill, some muffled, some hissing with malice. Virgil raises his hand.

*"This is Malebolge, the circle of fraud. Here are those who twisted trust for their gain, each punished in a ditch carved by their own deceit. Come — and learn how falsehood corrodes the chain as surely as fire corrodes the flesh."*
