## **Bolgia 10: The Falsifiers**

Here lies a plague. Souls cough and scratch, their skin diseased, their bodies collapsing into filth. The stench is unbearable.

*"These forged signatures, minted counterfeit certs, devised false algorithms. They claimed strength where there was none, counterfeited life itself. Now their own flesh rots, counterfeit of flesh, diseased and corrupted."*

He turns to you.

*"When attackers forged certificates for Google domains, or when fools built homegrown crypto — each was a falsehood, and each corroded trust. See them now, their very forms corrupted as their work once was."*

---

Virgil’s eyes are grim as he surveys the ditches.

*"Fraud is a hydra — ten thousand heads, ten thousand faces. Each trench holds one, but know this: all are the same at the root. Each took trust, and twisted it. Each made safety a lie. And each, in the end, is damned here, in Malebolge."*
