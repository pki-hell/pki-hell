# **Circle 5: Wrath – The River Styx, Revocation Denied**

The river ahead boils with filth. A dark mire stretches as far as the eye can see, its waters thick with rage. On the surface, men and women claw at one another, snarling, tearing, their limbs slick with mud. Beneath the surface, others lie motionless, their mouths and eyes sealed, suffocated in silence.

Virgil’s voice cuts through the stink.

---

*"Here are the wrathful — who raged in life against revocation. They hurled lists of distrust, too vast, too late, expired before they could save a soul. Now they fling these broken scrolls at one another, forever unread, forever unheeded."*

As you watch, one soul tears a fragment from a glowing list and hurls it across the mire. It lands in the mud, already rotting. Another soul scrambles to seize it, but the page has blackened and turned to sludge in his hands.

*"A CRL too long is no lifeline, but an anchor. A list not updated is not trust, but poison. Their fury built nothing but outages, and so they drown in their own lists."*

---

Virgil points lower, to the shapes trapped beneath the mire. You see only faint outlines, their mouths bubbling muck.

*"And here the sullen lie — those who would not publish, who claimed revocation did not matter. They denied the very need to withdraw trust, and so they are denied breath forever. They lie unmoving, for they chose silence. They choke in the filth they left unchecked."*

One shadow rises just enough for you to glimpse a face — eyes wide with despair — before it slips beneath the mire again.

---

The stench of the swamp grows stronger as you walk along the bank. You notice broken posts sticking up from the mud — each carved with strange, decaying letters: `ldap://`, `http://`, addresses fading into nothingness.

*"Behold the rotting CDPs and dead OCSP responders. They thought to anchor revocation to weak shores, and when those shores fell, all trust withered. When the endpoints are unreachable, the path of validation collapses, and the message drowns here."*

---

Virgil’s eyes burn as he looks across the swamp.

*"Mark this well, my student. Revocation is not debate, but duty. To neglect it is to poison your own river. To rage without reason is to drown in futility. To deny it is to be silenced in mud. All trust ends here, unless you learn to carry the lists with care, to refresh them in time, to anchor them where no flood can erase them."*
