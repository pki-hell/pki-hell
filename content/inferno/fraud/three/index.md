## **Bolgia 3: The Simoniacs**

Souls hang upside down in stone holes, their legs kicking wildly. Flames lick their feet, blackening the flesh.

*"These made trust a market. They sold certificates like indulgences, offering crowns of authority to any who paid. They did not verify, they did not protect — they sold signatures for coin. Now their heads are buried in stone, their feet set alight by their greed."*

He frowns.

*"Think of the early days of SSL — when a certificate could be bought for a few coins, no questions asked, no validation of identity. They crowned anyone who asked. Their flames still burn."*
