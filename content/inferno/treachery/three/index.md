## **Round 3: Ptolomea (Traitors to Guests)**

The souls here lie supine beneath the ice, their faces upward, but their eyes sealed shut by frozen tears.

*"These betrayed those who entrusted themselves as guests — the users who placed their faith in them. They ran CAs, but signed lies. They ran services, but let their certs rot. They betrayed the hospitality of trust, and so their eyes are frozen shut with tears they can never shed."*

**Real world echo:** Major CA compromises (DigiNotar, Symantec) — the betrayal of every relying party.
