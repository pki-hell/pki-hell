# **Circle 9: Treachery — The Frozen Lake Cocytus**

The heat of Malebolge fades. The ground hardens beneath your feet, until even the air is brittle. A vast lake stretches out, frozen solid, its surface gleaming like black glass. Souls are trapped beneath the ice, their faces twisted in agony. No fire burns here, only the still death of trust.

Virgil’s voice is low, heavy with finality.

*"This is Cocytus, the pit of treachery. Here are those who betrayed the deepest bonds — kin, country, benefactors, trust itself. In life, their lies froze faith; in death, they are frozen likewise, denied even the mercy of movement."*
