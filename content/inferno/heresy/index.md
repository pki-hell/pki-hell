# **Circle 6: Heresy – The City of Dis**

The black gates groan shut behind you. Ahead stretches a city of fire, its streets lined with tombs that glow from within. Flames pour through cracks in the lids, and each scream rises like smoke into the sulfurous air.

Virgil walks slowly between the rows, gesturing with his staff.

---

*"Here lie the heretics of trust — those who mocked the order of the chain, who issued in arrogance, who configured without care. Their denials burned the faith of many, and so their bones burn here without end."*

---

One tomb bursts open, its lid glowing like iron fresh from the forge. Inside, a soul writhes, clutching a scroll marked: **ENROLL \= Domain Admin**.

*"This one carved his templates with reckless hand, granting crowns to peasants, power to all who asked. One request, and the kingdom fell. Now his tomb is sealed by the very permission he unleashed."*

The lid slams shut, sparks spraying into the air.

---

Another row lies ahead, each tomb engraved with names once trusted in the world above: **DigiNotar, Symantec, WoSign**. Their names glow red-hot, cracking, but never fading.

*"These are the false prophets of authority. They bore the title of Certificate Authority yet betrayed it, signing lies, issuing false chains. Each misissuance was a blasphemy, and so their tombs burn as warnings to all."*

---

Virgil leads you past a smaller graveyard of tombs, where the flames sputter instead of roar. On their lids are etched malformed inscriptions: `ServerAuth`, `CodeSigning`, `ClientAuth` — mismatched, incomplete, broken.

*"Here lie those who denied purpose itself — who set extensions falsely, who swore encryption when they meant signing, who declared usage where none belonged. Their certs confused, their authority mocked, they now smolder in endless error."*

The flames flare briefly, illuminating the inscriptions, then gutter back down into embers.

---

Finally, at the far edge of the cemetery, you see tombs cracked wide open. Fire spills across the ground, unchecked. Inside, shadows scramble and claw their way upward, flames clinging to them like oil.

*"And here are those who opened the tombs of enrollment. Their templates bore no guard, no restriction; anyone could enter, anyone could claim a crown. Their gates became infernos, and now their tombs burn without boundary, their flames spilling forever outward."*

---

Virgil turns to you, eyes reflecting the fire.

*"Mark this well, my student. Authority is not a gift to all, but a trust for the few. Templates must be guarded, purposes declared true, issuance performed with faith. Deny these truths, and you will lie here, sealed in flame, your certs burning with you."*
